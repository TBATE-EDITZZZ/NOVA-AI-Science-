Motion

In an ideal constant-gravity model with no air resistance, horizontal velocity remains constant while vertical velocity changes at a constant rate. Real trajectories can differ because of drag, wind, lift and other conditions.

Cells

Cells are the basic structural and functional units of organisms. Eukaryotic cells contain membrane-bound compartments; cell structures vary among cell types and organisms.

Scientific models

A model isolates selected features of a system. Its assumptions determine where it is useful; a simplified model should not be mistaken for a complete description of nature.
