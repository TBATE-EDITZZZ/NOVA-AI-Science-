# Image attribution

`pillars-of-creation-comparison.webp` is the NASA Science image asset “Pillars of Creation (Hubble and Webb Images Side by Side.” Credit: NASA, ESA, CSA, STScI; image processing and institution credits are available on the [NASA source page](https://science.nasa.gov/asset/webb/pillars-of-creation-hubble-and-webb-images-side-by-side/). NOVA uses it as a credited educational media item; visual color in telescope images depends on wavelength/filter assignments.

The page text and original caption are authoritative for its specific image. Do not treat this image as a live observation.
